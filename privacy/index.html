Calisthenia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026
App: Calisthenia (com.sahandrez.calisthenia)

This policy covers the Calisthenia Android app only. It explains what data is collected, how it is used and who it is shared with.

Summary

Calisthenia has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, workout logs, progress, settings and purchase state are kept in a local save file on your device. The file is encrypted and is never uploaded by the app.

Android Auto Backup may copy the save file to your own Google account backup. This is controlled by Google and your device settings. You can also export your save as a code and import it on another phone; that code goes wherever you paste it.

Optional “Location & weather”

This feature is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. After a workout is logged, the app asks two third-party services over the internet:

  • ipwho.is finds an approximate city and country from your device’s IP address.
  • open-meteo.com gives the weather for that rounded position.

These services see your device’s IP address and the rounded coordinates, as with any web request. The app keeps only the city, country, a position rounded to about 1 km and the weather, on your device, together with that workout. It does not keep your IP address. It does not use GPS and does not request the Android location permission.

Optional purchases

The app offers one-time “Support” tiers and a “coffee” tip through Google Play Billing.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google. The app never sees your payment details.
  • The app asks Google Play which tiers your Google account owns, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new phone.
  • Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third parties

The only third parties the app talks to are ipwho.is, open-meteo.com and Google Play Billing.

Permissions

The app uses the Internet permission (for the weather lookup and Google Play Billing) and the Vibrate permission (for haptics).

Children

Calisthenia is not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the local save. There is nothing on a server to delete. Turning off “Location & weather” stops the lookups. Purchases are managed in your Google Play account.

Health note

Calisthenia is not medical advice.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Its host, GitHub Pages, may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
